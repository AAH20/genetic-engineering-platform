6. Core Modules Summary

🧬 CRISPR Design

Guide RNA design, off-target prediction, efficiency scoring. Tools: CHOPCHOP, CRISPOR, Cas-OFFinder, CRISPResso2.

NP-Hard SOTA: Rule Set 3

🔬 Protein Engineering

De novo design, structure prediction, stability analysis. Tools: AlphaFold2, ESMFold, RFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN.

NP-Hard SOTA: AlphaFold3

🧪 Synthetic Biology

Genetic circuit design, pathway optimization, DNA synthesis. Tools: Cello, SynBioCAD, JBEI-ICE, SBOL.

NP-Hard SOTA: Cello 2.0

💉 Gene Therapy

Vector design, delivery optimization, immune response. Tools: AAV, Lentivirus, LNP, SEND.

Bottleneck: AAV Capacity SOTA: VERVE-101

📊 Computational Genomics

Variant calling, genome assembly, haplotype inference. Tools: GATK, DeepVariant, BCFtools, FreeBayes.

NP-Hard SOTA: DeepVariant

⚗️ Metabolic Engineering

Flux balance analysis, pathway design, strain optimization. Tools: COBRApy, OptKnock, RetroPathRL.

NP-Hard SOTA: COBRApy

🔍 Single-Cell Genomics

scRNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, multi-omics. Tools: Scanpy, Seurat, Cell Ranger, Squidpy.

Bottleneck: Sparsity SOTA: Seurat v5

🧬 Epigenomics

DNA methylation, histone modifications, chromatin. Tools: Bismark, MACS2, SnapATAC2, ArchR.

Bottleneck: Bisulfite Conversion SOTA: EPInformer

🤖 AI/ML for Genomics

Protein LMs, variant effect, drug-target. Tools: ESM-2, AlphaMissense, Evo 2, DeepChem.

NP-Hard SOTA: Evo 2

🔗 Integration Layer

Knowledge graphs, event buses, multi-agent, digital twins. Tools: Neo4j, Kafka, LangGraph, Cognee.

SOTA: Evo 2 + KG
